# Collab Space Readiness — Product decisions

## Decisions

- Use a novel, feature-specific surface because this is an internal readiness fixture.
- Keep all data local and synthetic.
- Validate draft, review-ready and reset behavior at every configured viewport.

## Decision basis

- A product Surface Pack would add irrelevant layout constraints to an infrastructure fixture.
- The fixture exists to prove the workflow boundary, not a production page pattern.
