# AI Character Motion Transfer — Product decisions

## Decisions

- Expose exactly three models; do not describe internal model versions.
- "AI Character Motion Swap" is the approved marketing alias; both names may appear on the page.
- Steps on the product page must follow the tool order: model → reference video → photo → generate.
- Social platforms that may be named: TikTok, Reels, Shorts.
- No numeric limits (clip length, resolution, credits) are published until PM confirms them.

## Decision basis

- The current ENU draft page uses these names and this order, so the spec keeps them.
- Naming limits without confirmed values would create claims support cannot honour.
