# AI Character Motion Transfer — Intake

> Fixture status: this feature is a spec-only upstream source for the product-page
> generator. It stays at the `intake` stage; no React prototype has been requested.

## Problem

Creators want a still photo or an illustrated character to move like a reference video
without filming, rigging or frame-by-frame animation.

## Review goal

Confirm that the web tool spec below is complete enough to generate the product page:
model choices, the four-step flow, the three use cases, benefits and FAQ answers.

## Target user

Social creators and hobbyists on YouCam Online who already have a photo or character
image and a short reference clip, and who publish to TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

## Scope

In scope: choosing one of three models, uploading a reference video, uploading a photo,
generating, previewing, downloading in HD and finding the result in My Gallery.
Out of scope: audio or voice sync, multi-character scenes, 4K export, mobile-app-only
features, pricing details.

## Open product decisions

None for the product-page fixture. Real limits (clip length, resolution, credits) must be
added by PM before a production draft is published.

## Decision basis

The current ENU draft page already communicates this scope; the spec restates it so
generated content can cite a PM-owned source instead of the CMS export.
