# AI Character Motion Transfer

## Goal

Let a user apply the motion of a reference video to a still photo or character image,
or swap their character into an existing clip, entirely inside the YouCam Online web tool.

## Product names

- Official name: AI Character Motion Transfer
- Approved display alias for marketing copy: AI Character Motion Swap

## Models

The tool offers exactly three models. The user picks one before uploading.

1. Character Motion Control — apply the movement from a reference video to a still photo.
2. Replace the Character in a Video — swap the performer in a clip with the character from the photo while keeping the clip's motion, gestures and camera movement.
3. Apply Video Motion to Photo — animate an illustrated or AI-generated character with the motion of a reference clip.

## Tool flow

1. Choose a model.
2. Upload a motion reference video (a gesture, a performance, a dance).
3. Upload the photo to animate (a portrait, a character illustration, or any image with a clear subject).
4. Generate, preview the result, download it in HD, or find it saved in My Gallery.

## Benefits

- Natural motion: the character moves smoothly and realistically, matching the reference video.
- Still looks like you: faces, proportions and style carry through, so the result still looks like the original photo or character.
- Multiple AI models: three models, each tuned for a different result.
- No filming, rigging, 3D modeling or frame-by-frame drawing required.

## Use cases

- Make any photo move: pair a dance clip, a walking shot or a simple gesture with a photo; fits TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- Give any character real motion: animate illustrated or AI-generated characters for storytelling, avatars and character content while keeping art style consistent from first frame to last.
- Swap yourself into any scene: replace the original performer in an action clip while keeping the clip's motion, gestures and camera movement intact; fits memes, reaction videos and social content.

## FAQ

- What do I need to start? One photo with a clear subject and one reference video with the motion you want.
- Which model should I pick? Character Motion Control for moving a photo, Replace the Character in a Video to swap yourself into a clip, Apply Video Motion to Photo for illustrated or AI-generated characters.
- Does the result keep my face and style? Yes, faces, proportions and art style carry through from the original image.
- What kind of reference video works best? A clip with one clear subject and visible full-body or upper-body motion, such as a gesture, performance or dance.
- Can I animate an illustration or AI-generated character? Yes, use Apply Video Motion to Photo.
- Where is my result saved? Results can be downloaded in HD and are saved in My Gallery.
- Do I need animation or 3D skills? No, the tool needs only a photo and a reference video.
- Can I use it for social videos? Yes, results fit TikTok, Reels, Shorts, memes and reaction videos.
