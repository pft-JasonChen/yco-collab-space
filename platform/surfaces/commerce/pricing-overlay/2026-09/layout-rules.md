# Pricing overlay layout rules

Open above the current page from the authenticated header pricing icon. Preserve the
two-part RD composition: benefits on the left and plan selection on the right. Plan
selection may change local presentation state, but checkout remains inert in prototypes.

## Removed production dependencies

Redux modal state, pricing and subscription APIs, Stripe and 2Checkout, account
entitlements, analytics, holiday campaigns and remote currency data are excluded.
